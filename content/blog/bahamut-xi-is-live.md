Welcome to **Bahamut XI**, a Level 75 era FINAL FANTASY XI server.

We play the game as it was before *Wings of the Goddess*: the original nations, Rise of the Zilart, Chains of Promathia and Treasures of Aht Urhgan, with the level cap raised through Maat's trials and none of the later shortcuts. Era-accurate client data ships with the launcher, so menus, shops and dialogue match the time too.

## Getting in

1. [Create your account](/register) on this website.
2. Download the launcher for [Windows or Linux](/getting-started).
3. Sign in to the launcher with the same username and password, press **Play**, and make your character.

## Where to find us

Questions, party finding and news all happen on our Discord. Check the [rules](/rules) and the [approved addons](/approved-addons) before you set out.

See you in Vana'diel.
